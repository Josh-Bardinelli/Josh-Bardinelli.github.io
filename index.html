About Me

I'm a computer scientist that graduated from the University of Connecticut with a B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in mathematics, and an M.S. in computer science and engineering. Throughout my education and work experience I have built strong skills in programming, cybersecurity, information technology, and machine learning. I have more in-depth experience in systems programming, DNS, and virtualization.

My research on hyDNS, an accelerated DNS resolver using eBPF to run entirely in kernel space, was published at the SIGCOMM eBPF Workshop '24. Professionally, I have experience in cybersecurity work (threat investigation, EDR monitoring, application security) and building software, most recently implementing novel data linkage algorithms for AlgorithmicPro AI.

For personal projects, I enjoy creating and building web-based videogames. My current project is called JTrivia and is a Jeopardy-style trivia game. I am currently working on expanding the features, but the game is playable at JTrivia.app. I also enjoy working collaboratively on these projects with some of my friends who are also software developers.
